# Phase 05.3 Plan 04: Telegram outages stay invisible to the work — Summary

**Telegram trouble now stays out of the way.**

- Notifications wait and retry quietly: backoff from 5 s to 60 s, and 429 `retry_after` honored up to 300 s.
- An outage prints one warning line and one recovery line.
- The queue holds at most 200 items.
- A gate raised during an outage is delivered exactly once after recovery.

The outbox can now also edit messages and refresh buttons, for the button plans that follow.
