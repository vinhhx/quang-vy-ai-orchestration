# Phase 05.2 Plan 11: Autopilot driver — Summary

**`agent-brain auto` now drives a task by itself.** It claims the task, asks `decide()` for the next step, and runs each stage as a separate `agent-brain run` child. It stops at the gate, at a cap, at the budget, or when you stop or kill it. Ctrl+C, a soft stop and a Kill all leave the task in a resumable state, with no orphaned processes.
