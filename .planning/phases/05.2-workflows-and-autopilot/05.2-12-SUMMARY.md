# Phase 05.2 Plan 12: Kill stops autopilot — Summary

**`agent-brain kill TASK` (and the dashboard Kill) now stops the task's autopilot as well as the running step.** The driver ends with "dừng bởi bạn", and no process survives, not even when the driver itself was SIGKILLed. Without a driver, Kill behaves exactly as in 05.1.

## Deviations

- **Executor:** a Claude `gsd-executor` subagent in an isolated worktree, in place of agy (agy's quota is exhausted until 2026-10-24; your decision).
- **The executor's test choices, beyond the plan:**
  - `kill_in_gap_sets_flag` uses a `python3 time.sleep` stand-in instead of `sleep 60`.
  - `sigkilled_driver_leaves_nothing_running` also re-runs `auto` to `done`. This proves a task can be claimed again after its driver was SIGKILLed.
- **Race found by the executor, confirmed by Claude, fixed by FU-1.**
  - The race: on Kill, the driver also noticed the kill flag and called `cmd_kill` on the same run. If the tree was already gone, the unconditional `mark_status(.., "lost")` could overwrite the `killed` the run child had just written.
  - Follow-up plan `scratchpad/agy/052-12fu.plan.md` adds two guards: a guarded `mark_lost_if_running` in `cmd_kill`, and the driver deferring to an in-flight Kill (`kill_requested_at` set).
  - It also adds the test `kill_mid_run_records_killed_not_lost` (3 repetitions) and the first unit tests in `db.rs`.
- **Merge order:** plan 14 ran in parallel and was cherry-picked onto `main` between the plan-12 commits and FU-1, so FU-1 was cherry-picked too. The history is linear.
