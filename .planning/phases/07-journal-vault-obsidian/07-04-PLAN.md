New order of `sanitize_text` after this plan (insert steps, keep the rest):
1. NFC; drop control chars except `\n`, zero-width, bidi, BOM.
2. NEW: cut at the first line that starts (after optional spaces) with `diff --git `, `--- a/`, `+++ b/`, `@@ -`, `index ` followed by 7+ hex chars and `..`, or at the first triple backtick; drop it and everything after; append ` (đã bỏ phần mã)` when something was dropped.
3. PEM: from `-----BEGIN` to the end → ` (đã ẩn)`.
4. Whitespace runs to one space (one line).
5. Known values → `secrets::redact_str`.
6. NEW, in this order (each match replaced by the word in brackets): URLs `[a-z][a-z0-9+.-]*://\S+` and `www\.\S+` (liên kết); e-mails (email); `key|token|secret|password|passwd|pwd|api[_-]?key|access[_-]?key|authorization` followed by optional spaces, `:` or `=`, and a value (đã ẩn); `Bearer <8+ chars>` (đã ẩn); token shapes `sk-ant-…`, `sk-…`, `gh[pousr]_…`, `github_pat_…`, `glpat-…`, `xox[baprs]-…`, `AKIA[0-9A-Z]{16}`, `AIza[0-9A-Za-z_-]{35}`, JWT `eyJ….….`, Telegram bot `\d{6,}:[A-Za-z0-9_-]{30,}` (đã ẩn); IPv4 with optional `:port` and `localhost[:port]` (địa chỉ); bare hosts ending in `.com .net .org .vn .io .dev .app .local .internal .lan .corp .test .cloud .xyz .info .biz` with optional `:port` (địa chỉ); paths: Windows `[A-Za-z]:\\\S*`, home `~/\S*`, absolute tokens starting with `/` that have at least one more character, relative tokens containing `/` with a non-empty segment on both sides (đường dẫn); ALL_CAPS env-var names `[A-Z][A-Z0-9]*(_[A-Z0-9]+)+` (đã ẩn); tokens of 24+ chars from `[A-Za-z0-9+/=_-]` that contain at least one letter and one digit (đã ẩn).
7. Allow-list, collapse, trim, cut (unchanged from 07-02).

Gate rules for `scan_generated` (on every body and every path; vault files never contain URLs or absolute paths by construction, so these are high-signal): `known-value`; `url` (`[a-z][a-z0-9+.-]*://`); `abs-path` (`/home/`, `/root/`, `/mnt/`, `/Users/`, `~/`, a drive letter followed by `:\`); `pem` (`-----BEGIN`); `token-shape` (the token shapes of step 6); `jwt`; `telegram-token`; `key-assignment` (same rule as step 6); `email` (`[\w.+-]+@[\w-]+\.[\w.-]+`).
